# Feedback about Orbito

This file collects notes about **Orbito itself** — the tool and its skills — not about your product. Claude adds a short, anonymous entry when something did not go the way you wanted, when the tool or a skill failed or misled, when something was missing, or when something worked especially well.

- **Anonymous:** no names of people, companies, clients or products, no content or data from your screens. Check it before sending and remove anything you do not want to share.
- **Yours to edit:** add your own thoughts anywhere, delete what you disagree with.
- **Nothing is sent automatically.** When it feels like the right moment (end of a phase, end of the project), send this file to **orbito@uncool.pro**. Every note helps make the skills and the tool better for everyone.
