# Tells of the generated look

Last reviewed: 2026-10. Review this list every few months: tells change as models and fashions change.

A tell is a device that makes a screen read as generated. Most of them started as good ideas; they became tells by being used by default, everywhere, without a reason. This list is for proposing variants: avoid leaning on these unless the product's idea calls for one, and then say why. It is not an audit rule: a client may choose any of them on purpose.

It deliberately names no replacements ("use this font instead"). A list of replacements becomes the next set of tells. The replacement is always the procedure in `SKILL.md`: the product's inventory, an idea sentence, a dealt direction.

## Colour
- Violet or indigo gradients on backgrounds, buttons and glows; gradient-filled headline text.
- A dark ground with one neon accent and a glow around it.
- The "warm and premium" shortcut: a cream ground with a terracotta or rust accent and a serif headline. It was the remedy for the gradients and is now the most recognisable generated look.
- Colour spread over the chrome (sidebar, headers, icons) instead of on the data and the one main action.

## Surfaces and shapes
- A soft grey shadow under every card; glass panels with background blur.
- One medium corner radius on everything.
- A grid of rounded tiles of different sizes as the default way to show features.
- A floating pill-shaped navigation bar with blur.

## Type and labels
- A tiny uppercase letter-spaced label above every heading.
- A pill badge above the title ("New", "Beta").
- One word of a heading in a different style or colour.
- Many font sizes that are almost the same, so nothing leads.
- Monospace labels or numbering (01 / 02 / 03) as decoration.

## Content blocks
- Three identical cards, each with an icon in a tinted rounded square.
- A row of four KPI tiles, each with an icon in a coloured circle, a green "+12%" and a sparkline.
- "Welcome back" with a waving hand as the screen title.
- Emoji as icons or bullets.
- A fake chart or dashboard made of grey bars as an illustration.

## Motion
- The same fade-and-rise on every block; a hover lift on every card; bounce easing; endless decorative loops.

## Removing is not enough
A variant with every tell removed and nothing in their place is an empty variant, and that is a tell too. The tells were filling a gap where an idea should have been: fill it with the idea.
